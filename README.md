# Midway Drop
A game about dropping your balls

## How to Play

Move your pointer along the top of the board and click (or tap) to drop a ball. It bounces down through the peg field and lands in one of the scoring zones at the bottom. You start each session with 10 balls — the run ends when you're out.

## Rules

- **Scoring zones**: each zone at the bottom is worth points (100 / 500 / 1000 / 5000). Landing outside a zone (a total miss) scores 0.
- **Combo multiplier**: the two 1000 zones and the 5000 zone are "qualifying" landings. Land in one and your multiplier climbs by +0.5x (starting at 1x, capped at 3x), applied to your *next* scoring landing. Land in a low-value zone (100/500) or miss entirely and the multiplier hard-resets to 1x — watch for the red flash, that's your streak breaking.
- **Bonus balls**: extend your session by earning extra balls, up to 5 bonus balls per session, from three sources:
  - Landing in either far-outer zone (the hardest zones to hit) grants +1 ball.
  - Crossing every 500 points of score grants +1 ball — a single big multiplied hit can cross several thresholds at once and earns a ball for each.
  - Reaching a 3x combo grants +1 ball, but only the first time each session.
- **Near misses**: land just outside the top-value zone and the narrator will let you know how close you were.
- When your balls run out, your final score is compared against your saved high score.
